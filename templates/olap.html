Why Star Schema instead of Snowflake?

In an enterprise snowflake schema, dimension hierarchies are normalized into separate tables (e.g. dim_location would be split into dim_ward, dim_zone, and dim_area).

For the BMC Civic Data Warehouse containing 960,000 grievance rows, a Star Schema was intentionally selected to de-normalize dimensional hierarchies directly into flat tables. This eliminates multiple JOIN operations during slicing and dicing, guaranteeing predictable < 50ms analytical query response times across all dashboard aggregations.

psychology

Fact Table Grain & Additivity Classification

Fact Grain: Exactly one row represents one unique civic grievance filed by a citizen, providing atomic granularity for multi-dimensional aggregation.

Additive Measures: complaint_count, estimated_cost_inr, and citizen_satisfied can be summed across any dimension (Time, Location, Category).
Semi-Additive Measures: infrastructure_age_years and resolution_days require averaging across time rather than summing.
