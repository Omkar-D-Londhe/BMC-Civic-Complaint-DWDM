Why FP-Growth instead of Apriori?
Apriori generates candidate itemsets combinatorially (O(2|I|) candidate search space, where |I| is the item cardinality), requiring repeated multi-pass scans of 960,000 transactions. FP-Growth encodes frequent itemsets into a compact Frequent Pattern Tree (FP-Tree) in only 2 database passes with zero candidate generation.

How were the 24 wards clustered?
Wards were standardized via Z-score normalization across 8 indicators (Slum %, Mean Latency, Recurrence Rate, Monsoon Sensitivity, Volume). Silhouette score evaluation confirmed k=3 as the optimal global elbow.

Association Lift Interpretation:
Lift = P(B|A) / P(B). A lift of 2.71x proves that when low-severity drainage issues occur during monsoon, delayed resolution is 271% more likely than random baseline.
