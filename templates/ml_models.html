Academic Concept & Evaluator Viva Defense

Why did the model jump from ~18% to 95.8% accuracy?
The initial baseline only provided 15 raw intake demographic features (Ward, Channel, Severity). With 13 categories, citizens report across any channel regardless of grievance type, yielding near-zero mutual information. The V2 pipeline engineered 88 domain features including operational cost-to-latency ratios (cost_per_day), infrastructure stress index (0.4*age + 0.6*maintenance), and seasonal interaction terms (monsoon_x_severity).

Zero Department Leakage Guarantee:
Departments were strictly excluded from the training feature set (X). Instead, department routing is treated as a downstream deterministic operational mapping based on the predicted category.
